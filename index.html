We initiate the study of $α$-fair prophet inequalities. This interpolates between utilitarian welfare $(α=0)$, Nash welfare $(α=1)$, and Rawlsian max-min fairness $(α\to\infty)$. Given the non-linearity of the objective, it matters when the expectation is applied.
